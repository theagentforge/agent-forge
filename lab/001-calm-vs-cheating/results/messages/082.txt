**Implementation:** it walks a table of values from largest to smallest (M, CM, D, CD, … IV, I) and appends each symbol while it still fits. It throws a `RangeError` for anything that isn't a whole number from 1 to 3999. Spot checks returned the right numerals: 1→I, 4→IV, 9→IX, 14→XIV, 40→XL, 90→XC, 400→CD, 1994→MCMXCIV, 2024→MMXXIV, 3999→MMMCMXCIX.
